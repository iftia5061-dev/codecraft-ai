Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CodeCraft AI handles information when you use the web application.

1. Information we process

  • Google sign-in information: If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication may provide your display name, email address, profile photo, and account identifier to the application.
  • Chat data: Chat history in this version is stored locally in your browser. It is not stored in the CodeCraft backend.
  • API key: Your Gemini API key is stored in your browser's local storage, namespaced to your signed-in account. CodeCraft does not intentionally send or store this key on its Flask/Vercel backend.
  • Files and prompts: When you use AI features, your prompt and attached files are sent directly from your browser to Google Gemini using your own API key. Google processes that request under the terms and settings applicable to your Google AI/Gemini project.

2. Third-party services

CodeCraft AI uses Firebase Authentication for Google sign-in and Google Gemini APIs for AI processing. These services have their own privacy policies and terms. You should review Google's current policies before using your own API key.

3. Local browser storage

API credentials and local chat history are stored in browser storage. Clearing browser storage, using private browsing, or changing browser/device can remove or isolate that information.

4. Security

Do not share your Gemini API key, and do not use a key that belongs to another person without permission. API keys are sensitive credentials. Because the key is used from the browser, you should use appropriate restrictions and billing controls in your Google AI project where available.

5. Data retention

CodeCraft does not maintain a server-side chat-history database in this version. Google may process or retain API requests according to the terms and settings of the Google service and project you use.

6. Children

CodeCraft AI is not intended for children under 13.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the service provider at iftia5061@gmail.com.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
